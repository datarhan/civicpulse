# CivicPulse — Launch Marketing Kit (MVP)

**Date:** 29 June 2026 · **Launch town:** Riba-roja de Túria (~24,600 hab.)
**Launch set:** Quejas · Panel de gobierno (Cargos/Departamentos) · Promesas · Dinero público (Presupuesto + contratos)
**Tone:** Neutral, civic, factual. Never partisan. Every claim traceable to a public source. This is the brand's whole credibility.

> Note: data content (headlines, names, quotes, legal citations) stays in the source language for verbatim accuracy; chrome is bilingual ES / Valencià. Copy below is provided in Castilian Spanish with English notes for the team.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner (ES):**
> CivicPulse es el monitor cívico de Riba-roja de Túria: a dónde va el dinero público, qué prometieron los partidos y qué se cumple — con la fuente oficial detrás de cada dato.

**One-liner (EN, internal):**
> CivicPulse is the civic monitor for Riba-roja de Túria — where public money goes, what parties promised, and what got done, with the official source behind every figure.

**Category:** Local government transparency / open-data civic tech.

**What makes it different (the 3 proof points):**
1. **100% public, traceable data.** Every number links to its primary source (Hacienda, PLACSP, BDNS, INE, BOE, the town hall itself). Nothing invented.
2. **Verbatim + right-of-reply.** Promises and findings use the exact public quote, with a built-in way for any party to respond. Built like a newsroom, not a campaign.
3. **A complaint becomes an official act in 2 minutes.** The Quejas channel turns a citizen's photo of a pothole into a registered municipal request with a public legal clock.

## 2. Key messages (per launch feature)

**Dinero público / Presupuesto** — *"Sigue cada euro."*
€43.5M de ingresos, €41.6M de gasto, €1.689 por habitante. Un mapa de a dónde van las obras y quién recibe los contratos. Datos reales de CONPREL (Hacienda) y PLACSP.

**Promesas** — *"Lo que prometieron, palabra por palabra."*
16 compromisos públicos de PSOE, PP, VOX y Compromís, cada uno con la cita literal, la fuente y su estado. Con derecho de réplica para cada partido.

**Panel de gobierno / Cargos + Departamentos** — *"Quién decide y de qué responde."*
Alcalde y 20 concejales, sus carteras, su sueldo público (ISPA) y los acuerdos de pleno por concejalía. Rendición de cuentas, no opinión.

**Quejas ciudadanas** — *"Un bache, una denuncia, en 10 segundos."*
Envía `/queja` al bot de Telegram con foto y ubicación. Si 10 vecinos la apoyan, entra como solicitud oficial en el Registro Electrónico. Reloj legal público, sin coste, sin publicar tus datos personales.

## 4. Audience-tailored angles

- **Vecino/a (default):** "Denuncia en 10 segundos" + "¿a dónde va mi dinero?" Lead with Quejas and the budget map.
- **Periodista:** verbatim quotes, traceable sources, right-of-reply, open data you can cite. Lead with Promesas + Presupuesto + the methodology page.
- **Cargo municipal:** rendición de cuentas justa, datos oficiales, derecho de réplica integrado. Frame as fair and source-backed, not "gotcha."
- **Activista:** el reloj legal LPACAP, el escalado al Síndic de Greuges, los contratos por zona.

---

## 5. Launch channels & sequence

1. **Local press first** (Periòdic del Camp de Túria, infoturia, Levante-EMV, Las Provincias) — the press note above. They already cover Riba-roja and value the source-traceability.
2. **Telegram / WhatsApp neighborhood groups** — the community blurb + the bot link. This is also how Quejas gets its first real data.
3. **Social** (X, Bluesky, Instagram) — the short post, ideally with a 10-second screen recording of the budget map and a queja being filed.
4. **Town-hall-adjacent civic associations** (AAVV, comerciantes) — the audience-tailored "vecino" angle.

---

## 6. Guardrails for all marketing (non-negotiable)

- **Never partisan.** Don't frame any feature as "exposing" a specific party. The product is a mirror, not a megaphone. Equal framing for all four groups.
- **Don't over-claim Quejas.** Until real complaints come in, market it as "open — be the first," not "see what your neighbors reported."
- **Don't reference Periodistas IA / AI biographies** in any launch material. That surface is intentionally hidden for launch.
- **Always show the source.** "Datos oficiales, fuente en cada cifra" is the line that earns trust — use it everywhere.
- **Lead with the citizen benefit,** not the tech. People care about potholes and money, not scrapers.

---

## 7. Suggested launch-day metrics to watch

- First quejas submitted via the bot (the real activation signal).
- Unique visitors to `/presupuesto` and `/promesas` (the shareable surfaces).
- Local-press pickups and inbound right-of-reply requests (a sign you're being taken seriously).
